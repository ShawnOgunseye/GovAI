Back Cover

How to Govern AI · Second Edition

Every organization running AI is already governing it, badly, informally, or by accident. This book is about doing it on purpose.

It moves from what AI governance actually is, through the full lifecycle of a model from scoping to retirement, into the harder territory of generative and agentic systems, and out to the org design, risk practice, and evidence trail that make a governance function real rather than a slide deck.
