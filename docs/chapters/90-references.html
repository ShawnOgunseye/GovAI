References

Sources are grouped by type, because the verification each type needs differs. Legislation is cited by instrument number and, where a provision carries the argument, by article. Standards are cited by number and year. Enforcement actions and litigation are cited by case name, court, docket, and the date of the operative order, since the order rather than the coverage of it is what the book relies on. Scholarship is cited in full.

Where a source moves faster than a printed book can, the text says so in place rather than only here. Statutory effective dates and standards publication statuses below were confirmed in September 2026 and should be re-checked before being relied on.

Legislation and regulation

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Artificial Intelligence Act). Cited throughout Chapter 2, principally Articles 5, 6, and 9 through 15, and Annexes I and III.

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, amending Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Digital Omnibus on AI). Official Journal, 24 July 2026; in force 27 July 2026.

Regulation (EU) 2016/679, General Data Protection Regulation. Principally Articles 5, 6, 9, 22, and 35.

California SB 53, frontier developer safety framework disclosure. Effective 1 January 2026.

Colorado SB 26-189, Automated Decision-Making Technology Act. Effective 1 January 2027. Repealed SB 24-205 on 14 May 2026, before that act’s own deferred start date.

Illinois HB 3773, amending the Illinois Human Rights Act. Effective 1 January 2026.

New York RAISE Act, frontier model transparency. Effective 1 January 2027.

Texas HB 149, Responsible Artificial Intelligence Governance Act. Effective 1 January 2026. Government-use disclosure and prohibitions, enforced by the Attorney General; it is not an algorithmic-discrimination duty-of-care statute and does not reach private employment.

New York City Local Law 144 of 2021, automated employment decision tools. Bias audit, published summary, and candidate notice.

Civil Rights Act of 1964, Title VII; Americans with Disabilities Act; Age Discrimination in Employment Act; Equal Credit Opportunity Act; Fair Credit Reporting Act; Fair Housing Act.

Infocomm Media Development Authority (Singapore). Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI, version 1.5, 20 May 2026.

Enforcement actions and litigation

Williams v. City of Detroit, No. 2:21-cv-10827 (E.D. Mich.). Final Order of Dismissal and Settlement Agreement entered 28 June 2024. The operative governance terms cited in Chapter 1 are Detroit Police Department Manual Directives 307.5 and 203.11, incorporated by the order, together with the retrospective audit requirement and the court’s retention of jurisdiction to 28 June 2028.

Federal Trade Commission v. Rite Aid Corporation, No. 2:23-cv-05023 (E.D. Pa.). Complaint filed 19 December 2023; Stipulated Order for Permanent Injunction entered 26 February 2024. Provision II.A, deletion of photographs, videos, and any data, models, or algorithms derived from them; five-year facial recognition ban; twenty-year order term. Quantified allegations cited in the text are at complaint paragraphs 31, 41, 50, 51, and 139.

Equal Employment Opportunity Commission v. iTutorGroup, Inc. Settled 2023 by public consent decree, $365,000. The allegation concerned application software that automatically rejected older applicants. Whether the screening tool used machine learning was never established on the record.

Journalism as documenting source

Where no primary document exists, the book says so in the text and names the reporting rather than treating it as established fact.

Dastin, J. (2018, 10 October). Amazon scraps secret AI recruiting tool that showed bias against women. Reuters. The sole source for the Amazon recruiting tool. No primary document, regulatory action, litigation, or company confirmation exists; the account rests on anonymous sources, and Chapter 1 presents it on those terms.

Hill, K. (2020, 24 June). Wrongfully accused by an algorithm. The New York Times. The reporting that first documented the Williams arrest, cited alongside the settlement rather than in place of it.
